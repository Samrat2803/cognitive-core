4 facts discovered in chronological order

#1
The contaminated cough syrup contained nearly 45% diethylene glycol (DEG).
#2
The permissible limit of diethylene glycol in pharmaceuticals is 0.1%.
#3
Diethylene glycol (DEG) is not permitted as a solvent in pharmaceutical preparations.
#4
Reports of DEG contamination highlight ongoing regulatory failures in drug manufacturing.
